## Specimen requirements
topics: tissue requirements, specimen, workflow burden, sample, ffpe
headline: It runs on FFPE tumor tissue together with a matched normal blood or saliva sample from the same patient.

xT CDx analyzes DNA isolated from formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) tumor tissue specimens with a matched normal blood or saliva specimen from the same patient.
