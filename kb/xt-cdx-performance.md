## MSI concordance against IHC
topics: msi, biomarker, immunotherapy, evidence quality, accuracy, performance, concordance
headline: Against IHC, xT CDx showed 94.0% positive percent agreement and 98% negative percent agreement for MSI status.

Of the 117 samples identified as positive by IHC testing (Abnormal IHC, loss), 110 were identified as MSI-H by xT CDx, yielding a PPA of 94.0% (95% CI: 88-98%). Of the 199 samples identified as negative by IHC testing (Normal IHC, intact), 195 were identified as MSS by xT CDx, yielding a NPA of 98% (95% CI: 95-99%).

## KRAS accuracy in colorectal cancer
topics: colorectal, kras, accuracy, performance, evidence quality, concordance, therapy
headline: For KRAS in colorectal cancer, xT CDx showed 100% positive percent agreement and 99.83% negative percent agreement against an externally validated NGS assay.

Of the 30 CDx variants in KRAS that were positively identified by OM1, 30 were identified by xT CDx, yielding a PPA of 100% (95% CI: 88.43-100.0%). Of the 591 CDx variants in KRAS identified as negative by OM1, 590 were identified as negative by xT CDx, yielding a NPA of 99.83% (95% CI: 99.06-100.0%).

## Overall companion diagnostic accuracy
topics: accuracy, performance, evidence quality, concordance, clinical utility
headline: Across companion diagnostic variants, xT CDx showed 100% positive percent agreement and 99.8% negative percent agreement.

Of the 31 CDx variants positively identified by OM1, 31 were identified by xT CDx, yielding a PPA of 100% (95% CI: 88.8-100.0%). Of the 649 CDx variants identified as negative by OM1, 648 were identified as negative by xT CDx, yielding a NPA of 99.8% (95% CI: 99.1-100.0%).

## Contamination control
topics: quality, evidence quality, performance, reproducibility, workflow burden
headline: Across all replicates, germline mutation agreement was 100%, indicating no sample carryover or cross-contamination.

Across all replicates, the overall percent agreement of germline mutations was 100% indicating no sample carryover or cross-contamination.
