## Blood-based alternative
topics: tissue requirements, liquid biopsy, ctdna, specimen, workflow burden
headline: When tumor tissue is insufficient, Tempus xF analyzes circulating tumor DNA from a standard blood draw.

Tempus xF is a liquid biopsy assay that analyzes circulating tumor DNA from a peripheral blood draw, which may be considered when tumor tissue is insufficient or unavailable.
